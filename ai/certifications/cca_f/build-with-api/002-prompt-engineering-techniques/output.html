# One-Day Meal Plan for High-Performance Athlete

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete aiming to maximize performance and recovery for morning strength training and evening basketball:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,500 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (210g), Fat (115g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM) - Pre-Strength Training:** 
  * Pancakes (100g dry flour) with honey (40g), 3 whole eggs (150g), turkey sausage (100g), and orange juice (250ml)
  * Protein: 35g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 75g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (9:30 AM) - Post-Strength Training:**
  * Chocolate protein shake with banana (120g), whole milk (240ml), peanut butter (30g), and whey protein powder (30g)
  * Protein: 40g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 55g

* **Lunch (12:30 PM):**
  * Grilled lean beef (180g), brown rice (150g cooked), steamed carrots (100g), and olive oil drizzle (15ml)
  * Protein: 50g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 65g

* **Afternoon Snack (3:30 PM) - Pre-Basketball Game:**
  * Greek yogurt (200g, full-fat), granola (50g), and mixed berries (80g)
  * Protein: 25g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 55g

* **Pre-Game Snack (5:30 PM - 2 hours before game):**
  * White bagel (85g) with honey (20g) and sliced turkey (60g)
  * Protein: 20g, Fat: 5g, Carbs: 60g

* **Dinner (8:30 PM - Post-Basketball Game):**
  * Grilled salmon (160g), sweet potato (200g), asparagus (150g), and butter (10g)
  * Protein: 35g, Fat: 20g, Carbs: 50g

* **Evening Snack (10:30 PM):**
  * Cottage cheese (150g, full-fat) with almonds (25g)
  * Protein: 30g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 10g
