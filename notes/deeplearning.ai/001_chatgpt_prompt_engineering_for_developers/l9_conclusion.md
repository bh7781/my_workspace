# Key Takeaways from the Course

The course concludes by reinforcing the major concepts covered throughout the material.

Two core prompting principles are emphasized again:

1. Write clear and specific instructions
2. Give the model time to think when appropriate

These principles form the foundation of effective prompt engineering.

---

# Prompt Engineering as an Iterative Process

Another major takeaway:
> Good prompting is iterative.

The important skill is not:
- discovering a perfect prompt immediately

but instead:
- refining prompts systematically
- experimenting
- improving outputs over time

The discussion strongly reinforces:
> Having a process for prompt development is more important than memorizing prompt templates.
